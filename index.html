Three rules. Remember them.

The safest habits are simple.

Read the domain, not the message

A familiar logo or sender name can be copied. The domain is harder to fake convincingly.

Treat urgency as a warning

Scammers rush you so you act before checking. Stop when a message threatens a deadline or account closure.

Open official sites yourself

For banks, deliveries, and government services, type the known address or use the official app.
